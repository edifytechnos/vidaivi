# Tamil Nadu Class 12 Chemistry — which questions came from a real board paper

**The rule, unchanged:** a question is tagged with a year only if it was read
verbatim out of a full Tamil Nadu **HSE Second Year** Chemistry board paper for
that year, with its Q.P. code. Nothing is tagged from a guide, a model paper or
memory.

## The corpus

Three papers, all the **March main sitting**, fetched from the DGE Tamil Nadu
question bank and stored in `papers/tn12/`. Provenance per file is in
`papers/tn12/SOURCES.md`.

| Year | Q.P. code | File |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 6622 | `papers/tn12/chemistry-2023.pdf` |
| 2024 | 7422 | `papers/tn12/chemistry-2024.pdf` |
| 2025 | 8322 | `papers/tn12/chemistry-2025.pdf` |

The papers do not print their own year. It comes from DGE's label on the
combined PDF each was cut from, and each year has its own Q.P. series
(66xx / 74xx / 83xx), so the three cannot be confused.

**The tag reads `TN HSE Mar <year>`** — the board, the kind of paper (the March
main sitting, not a supplementary or a model paper) and the year.

## Answers were worked out, never copied

No answer key was used; each was solved independently. Four worth naming:

- **Assertion: F₂ has a greater bond dissociation energy than Cl₂ (2025 Q7).**
  Both halves are false. F–F is about 158 kJ/mol against 242 for Cl–Cl, and the
  lone-pair repulsion the Reason gives to chlorine is really fluorine's — it is
  what weakens the F–F bond.
- **Kw at 40 °C (2025 Q10).** Ionisation of water is endothermic, so Kw rises
  with temperature; 2.71×10⁻¹⁴ is the only option above 1.00×10⁻¹⁴.
- **1.73 BM (2023 Q4).** One unpaired electron: Cu²⁺ is d⁹. [CoCl₆]⁴⁻ has three,
  TiCl₄ none, and square planar [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ none.
- **Aluminium, ccp, r = 125 pm (2024 Q22).** a = 2√2 r = 353.6 pm.

## Changes of form, stated plainly

- Two paper MCQs are asked as **typed numbers**, because the app grades a number
  directly: the moles of I₂ from one mole of dichromate (3), and the oxidation
  number of Pt in [Pt(NO₂)(H₂O)(NH₃)₂]Br (+2).
- *"Can Fe³⁺ oxidise bromide to bromine?"* (2024 Q23) is a **short answer** that
  expects **No**; the paper wants the reasoning, which the worked solution gives.
- Paper answers that ask for a unit now say which one (*"in pm"*, *"in seconds"*).
- Degree signs, subscripts and charges lost in extraction are re-typeset in
  KaTeX. No question's content was changed.
- Two questions were left out on purpose: 2025's non-stick cookware question
  prints its answer as *tetrafluoro**ethane*** (the monomer is tetrafluoro**ethene**),
  and 2025's protein secondary-structure question has two defensible options.
  Copying either would put the paper's own flaw in front of a class.

## The Tamil medium

The papers are bilingual, but their Tamil is set in a legacy font encoding that
extracts as unreadable characters. So the Tamil-medium shelf
(`content/tn12-chemistry-ta/`) carries **our translation of the paper's English
wording**, with the same tag. The question is the one the board set; the Tamil
sentence is not the board's own.

## If a tag looks wrong

Correct it in the chapter's JSON under `content/tn12-chemistry/` and re-run
`node scripts/seed-library.mjs content/tn12-chemistry`.
