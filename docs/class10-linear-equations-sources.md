Chapter Test 3** (`lib-c10-linear-equations`,
`content/class10-maths/03-pair-of-linear-equations.json`).

Same rule and same corpus as Real Numbers — see
`docs/class10-real-numbers-sources.md`: 45 full CBSE Mathematics (Standard)
papers (2024, 2025, 2026). A question is tagged only if it was read verbatim out
of one of them.

## Untagged questions

Questions **7**, **8**, **9**, **14** and **15** carry no year: the condition for
a unique solution, elimination, a sum-and-difference problem, an age problem,
and the condition for infinitely many solutions. All are ordinary NCERT
Chapter 3 material, written to fill out the board pattern where the corpus had
no question with legible options.

## Changes of form, stated plainly

- Question **12** is 3 marks in the paper and keeps that weight here; question
  **13** keeps its 5.
- Questions **8** and **9** are asked as numeric entry with a single value to
  type, since the app grades a typed number directly.
- Maths is re-typeset in KaTeX. No question's content was changed.

Every answer was recomputed independently in Python — including Vijay's
₹12,000 / ₹10,000 and Rehana's 10 / 15 notes.
