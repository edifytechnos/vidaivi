# Tamil Nadu Class 12 Bio-Botany and Bio-Zoology — which questions came from a real board paper

**The rule, unchanged:** a question carries a year only if it was read verbatim
out of a full Tamil Nadu **HSE Second Year** board paper for that year.

## The corpus

The **Biology** paper (Q.P. 6627 / 7427 / 8327, March 2023 / 2024 / 2025),
split into Part I (Bio-Botany) and Part II (Bio-Zoology) — `papers/tn12/bio-*.pdf`,
provenance in `papers/tn12/SOURCES.md`. Every page was rendered as an image and
read; the papers print Tamil and English side by side, so the Tamil-medium
shelves carry the board's own Tamil for every tagged question.

## What is tagged

Every board MCQ is used, word for word, plus one 5-mark board question as each
chapter's long answer where the chapter has one. 2- and 3-mark written board
questions are descriptive and cannot be auto-marked, so they are not used.

| Shelf | Tagged | Of |
|---|---|---|
| Bio-Botany (English and Tamil, each) | 30 | 150 |
| Bio-Zoology (English and Tamil, each) | 31 | 195 |

## Left out on purpose

- **Botany 2023 Q4 / 2025 Q5** are the same "virus-free plants" MCQ — tagged once, 2023.
- **Zoology 2024 Q6** ("Biogenesis was coined by") — sources disagree between
  Henry Bastian and Thomas Huxley, so it is not used.
- Second 5-mark questions in a chapter, since a chapter has one long answer.

## Kept as printed

- Botany 2023 Q1 option (c) reads oddly; the question is still answerable.
- Zoology 2025 Q7 option (d) differs between languages ("Penicillium citrinum" /
  "பெனிசிலியம் சிற்றினம்"); each shelf shows its own language's print.

The Tamil wording of the questions that are not from a paper is a translation
and has not yet been checked by a Tamil-medium teacher.
