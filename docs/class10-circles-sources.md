## Why half this chapter is untagged

Circles, like Triangles, is dominated by **"In the given figure…"** questions:
tangents meeting at a labelled point with one angle marked, two circles with a
common tangent, a triangle circumscribing a circle with lengths on the diagram.
The app cannot show a diagram inside a question yet, so those were left out
rather than reworded into something the paper did not ask.

Examples found and skipped for this reason: $AT$ tangent with $\angle CAT = 40°$
(2024, 30/2/1 Q17), $QR$ a common tangent to two circles (2024, 30/4/1 Q18),
$AB$ and $AC$ tangents with $\angle ABC = 42°$ (2024, 30/4/2 Q9), $\triangle ABC$
circumscribing a circle (2024, 30/4/3 Q21), and $PQ$ tangent with
$\angle SQT = 55°$ (2026, 30/5/1 Q4).

What survives with a year on it is mostly the **prove that** questions, whose
statements are written in words.

## Untagged questions

Questions **3**–**8**, **14** and **15** carry no year: the length of a tangent
from 13 cm away, how many tangents from an interior point, the supplementary
relationship at $\angle APB = 60°$, the right angle between tangent and radius,
a chord of the larger of two concentric circles, the perimeter of a triangle
with an inscribed circle, Pythagoras on a tangent, and the $AB + CD = AD + BC$
result. All ordinary NCERT Chapter 10 material.

## Changes of form, stated plainly

- Two questions are asked as numeric entry so the app can grade a typed number.
- Maths is re-typeset in KaTeX. No question's content was changed.

Every numeric answer was recomputed independently in Python: $10\sqrt{2}$,
$4\sqrt{2}$, 12 cm, 8 cm and a perimeter of 30 cm.
