# Class 10 · Real Numbers — where each question came from

The test **Class 10 · Real Numbers — Chapter Test 1** (`c10-real-numbers-1`)
carries a `source` tag on the questions taken from a CBSE board paper. This file
is the evidence behind those tags, so the teacher can check them before the test
reaches a class.

**The rule used:** a question is tagged with a year only if it was read verbatim
out of a full question paper for that year, with its set code. Anything that
could not be pinned to a paper is in the test **untagged**, as ordinary
NCERT-based practice — no tag was invented to fill a slot.

Papers were read as text (`pdftotext`) from full-paper PDFs, all
**Mathematics (Standard)**. Class 10 Real Numbers questions sit in Section A
(1 mark), Section B (2 marks) and Section C (3 marks); the chapter does not carry
5-mark Section D questions, which is why the long answers here are worth 2 and 3.

## Changes of form, stated plainly

- Two questions that are MCQs in the paper (**10**, and the mark split on **9**)
  are asked here as numeric-entry, because the app grades a typed number
  directly. The wording and the answer are the paper's.
- Assertion–Reason options are written out in full rather than abbreviated, so a
  student reading on a phone does not have to hold the four letters in mind.
- Maths is re-typeset in KaTeX. Nothing about a question's content was changed.

## If a tag looks wrong

Correct it in the editor: every question now has a **Source** field beside its
Topic. Clearing the field removes the chip.
