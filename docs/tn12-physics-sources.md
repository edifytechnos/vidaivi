verbatim out of a full Tamil Nadu **HSE Second Year** Physics board paper for

## Symbols the text lost, and how they were recovered

Text extraction drops fractions, radicals and some superscripts, so three
questions were read from the **rendered page** before being used:

- **2024 Q9 (series RL circuit).** The options extract as "6, 4, Zero, 2"; the
  page shows π/6, π/4, 0, π/2.
- **2023 Q10 (maximum angle of refraction).** The refractive index extracts as
  "2", which could have been a √2 with its radical lost — that would change the
  answer from 30° to 45°. The page shows a plain **2**.
- **2025 Q5 (a dimension).** The expression vanished entirely; the page shows
  1/(μ₀ε₀). Option (c) extracts as [L⁻² T] and is really [L⁻² T²].

Questions whose options were fractions and were **not** checked against the
page were left out rather than guessed — among them the polaroid intensity, the
de Broglie ratio and the bent bar magnet.

## Answers were worked out, never copied

- **Two touching balls (2024 Q10).** The force is *more* than before because
  ((q₁+q₂)/2)² − q₁q₂ = ((q₁−q₂)/2)² ≥ 0 — equal only when q₁ = q₂.
- **Area and distance both doubled (2023 Q8).** C, Q and V are unchanged, but
  E = V/d halves, so the energy density falls to a quarter.
- **Threshold wavelength, φ = 3.313 eV (2024 Q12).** 375 nm (3750 Å).
- **GaAsP LED, 1.875 eV (2024 Q33).** 660 nm, red.

## Changes of form, stated plainly

- Paper MCQs asked as **typed numbers**: the toaster's power, the transformer's
  secondary current, the threshold wavelength.
- *"Will photoelectrons be observed?"* (2025 Q26) expects **Yes**; the worked
  solution gives the justification the paper asks for.
- Where a paper question asked for two quantities (2025 Q33: angular momentum
  *and* velocity), one is asked and the solution gives both.
- Answers are asked in a stated unit (pF, mV, nm, units of 10⁻⁵ m/s) so a typed
  number can be graded.
- Two questions were left out on purpose: 2025's ray-optics distance, whose
  answer depends on whether a²/λ or a²/2λ is taken, and 2025's Brewster plate
  angle, which turns on how "with respect to the horizontal" is read.

## The Tamil medium

The page images carry the board's own Tamil in a readable font, so the
Tamil-medium shelf's tagged questions can use **the board's own Tamil
wording**, read from the page, rather than a translation.
