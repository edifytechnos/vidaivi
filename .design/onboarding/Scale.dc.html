How it scales
1 One rule decides the control. Up to about six subjects, cards — you can read them all at a glance. Past that, the same step renders a search box and result rows. The flow, the wording and Step 2 never change.
2 Nobody scrolls 1,248 rows. A teacher knows what they teach, so typing beats browsing. Facets narrow without forcing an order, and “because you already teach CBSE” puts the likely answer on top before a key is pressed.
! The real problem at 1000+ is the data, not the layout. Today this dialog fetches every subject and every built-in test to open — at 1,248 subjects that is roughly 15,000 test records in one response, on a cheap Android phone. Search has to move to the server (a query with a limit), and the test list must load only for the subject actually picked.
