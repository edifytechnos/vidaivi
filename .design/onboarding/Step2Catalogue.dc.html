The recommendation
1 Do not make onboarding carry the shop. A teacher creating their first subject wants to start teaching, not to shop. Keep the official free set first and pre-selectable in one click; let the paid catalogue be something they scroll to, or reach later from + in the tests tree.
2 One tray, one total, one action. Free and paid in the same selection, summed once. Two parallel flows — tick the free ones, buy the paid ones separately — is how carts get abandoned.
3 Nobody buys a test they cannot see. Every paid row needs a preview of a few real questions. Without it the rating is the only signal, and a new author has none — which is how a marketplace fails to start.
