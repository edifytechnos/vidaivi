One row, six states — and what each one needs stored
1 · Free, not yet taken
Real Numbers Chapter Test 1 · 15 questions · 26 marks VIDAI ★ 4.8 · used by 2,140 classes FreePreview
Ticking it copies it. This is the only state that exists today.
2 · Paid, not owned
Real Numbers — Board Pattern Mock 80 marks · 3 hours · 38 questions by R. Krishnan · ★ 4.9 · used by 312 classes ₹99Preview 5 free
Ticking it adds to the total rather than copying. The preview is not optional — a new author has no rating, so without sample questions there is no reason to trust them.
3 · Paid and owned, not in this subject
Trigonometry — 50 MCQ Rapid Fire 50 questions · 50 marks by M. Iqbal · ★ 4.6 OWNED
Bought once, usable in every subject you teach. A teacher with two Class 10 sections must not pay twice — which means a licence is a row about (buyer, test), separate from any copy.
4 · Already in this subject
Statistics — Chapter Test 15 questions · 26 marks VIDAI already in this subject Added
Matched on copiedFrom, which is already stored on every copy today.
5 · The author has fixed something since you copied it
Quadratic Equations — Weak-area Drill 30 questions · 30 marks by S. Priya · 2 questions corrected since your copy Review changes
The one nobody designs until it bites. A copy is the teacher's to edit, so an update can never be forced onto it — but a wrong answer in a paid test that the author has already fixed, still being set to a class, is the kind of thing that ends a marketplace's reputation. Show it; let them choose.
6 · Free, from another teacher
Circles — Quick Revision 12 questions · 20 marks by A. Sharma · ★ 4.2 · used by 41 classes FreePreview
Same as 1, but the byline is a person rather than Vidai — which is why who wrote this has to be a first-class field, not a label hardcoded to "built in".
What the model needs that it has not got
1 On the test: author (a display name and an id, not just platform: true), price, listed, and counters for rating and usage. Cheap to add now while every test is Vidai's and free; expensive to backfill across a live library later.
2 A new table — licences, keyed by buyer so "what do I own" is one partition read. Ownership is not the copy: one purchase, many subjects, and the copy can be deleted without losing what was paid for.
3 A version on the master, so state 5 is answerable at all. copiedFrom already records the parent; a copy would also keep the version it was taken at.
! And the one that has to come first: tests and subjects still use a constant PartitionKey, so every read scans the whole table. A catalogue of 412 tests across hundreds of authors is not reachable from there. CLAUDE.md already names re-partitioning by owner as the outstanding architectural change — this is what makes it urgent rather than tidy.
