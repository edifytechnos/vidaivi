## Creating a login

**Students** → add. You get a username and a password on the screen; send them on
WhatsApp.

The password is shown **once** — it is stored scrambled and cannot be read back.
If it goes missing, reset it. That signs the student out everywhere, which is
exactly what you want if somebody else has the old one.

Passwords are three ordinary words and a two-digit number, all lowercase,
because a student reads it off a phone and types it on a phone.

## Approval

Creating a student needs you on the teacher allowlist. Before that you get
*waiting for approval*, not a refusal — an admin adds you and it works. Writing
tests does not wait on this.

## Seats

{{rules.teacherFreeStudents}} students are included; more are paid seats. A
refusal names the number you hit and what lifts it.

## Who sees a test

Publishing shares a test with **everyone you teach**. That is the default.

To narrow it: **Who sees this** in the editor, or **Seen by** in My tests. Pick
named students instead, and only they see it. A narrowed test does not even light
up its subject card for the others.

You can only assign your own students to your own test.

> A draft reaches nobody, whoever it is assigned to. If a test has vanished for
> your class, check it is published before anything else.

## A student's report

**Students** → a student. You get every paper they have handed in, with the mark,
the date and what to do next.

- **Open & mark** goes into the paper itself.
- **View paper** reads a finished one without marking.
- **See what they have so far** appears on a paper still in progress — those show
  with no score and no result chip, because a running subtotal is not a mark
  anybody has earned yet.
- **Give another attempt** hands one more go on a test that is capped.

The class average and the counts are over **handed-in** papers only, so an
unfinished one never drags the class down.

## Attempts on ready-made tests

A test copied from the library can be sat **{{rules.subjectAttempts}} times**.
Your own tests are uncapped. Handing in spends an attempt; starting one does not.

Out of attempts, the student keeps their marks, their answers and every worked
solution. Only the retake goes.

## Removing a student

It deletes the login, their attempts, their marks **and the photographs of their
handwriting**, and cannot be undone. Do it for a student who has genuinely left.
