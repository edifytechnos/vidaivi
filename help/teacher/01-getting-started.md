## Signing in

**Continue with Google** on [vidai.seyali.app](/). The first time you are asked
two things, once:

1. **Teacher or parent?** Pick **teacher**. It cannot be changed afterwards
   without an admin resetting the account, so read both tiles.
2. **A WhatsApp number**, for score reports.

## Two different gates, and they catch people out

Anyone may call themselves a teacher and start a trial. Writing tests, taking
library subjects and previewing everything works immediately.

**Issuing a login to a real child is separate.** That needs an admin to put you
on the teacher allowlist. Until they do, creating a student says *waiting for
approval* — that is not a failure, it is the approval step. Everything else keeps
working while you wait.

## The shape of the app

Everything hangs off **Your subjects**. A subject holds tests; a test holds
questions; students sit the tests you publish.

| Where | What it is |
|---|---|
| Your subjects | The subjects you own. The way in to everything. |
| Browse tests | The ready-made library, read-only, to judge before you take. |
| My tests | A table of every test you own, with publish and audience. |
| Students | Your roster, and each student's report. |
| To mark | Papers waiting on you. |

## The first ten minutes

1. **New subject** → your board → your class → tick the subjects you teach →
   tick the chapters you are teaching this month. They land as your own
   editable drafts.
2. Open one, read it, publish it.
3. Add your students (once you are approved) and send them their logins.
4. They sit it. You mark it. You release it.
