## The one press

On your Children screen, a paper with answers waiting shows **Mark N & release**.
Press it and three things happen:

1. Every unmarked written answer is read and given a mark.
2. Those marks are saved as **your** marks.
3. The paper is released, so your child can now see everything.

It takes a few seconds per answer. Leave the screen open while it runs.

## The marks are yours, not the machine's

Everywhere else in Vidai the AI only *proposes* and a teacher awards. Here you are
the marker and the button acts on your behalf — because the alternative is not a
person marking it, it is nobody marking it.

So: **every mark is yours to change.** Open the paper and you can raise or lower
any of them and add a comment. Where the number came from is kept beside it, so
you can always see what was suggested against what you gave.

Read at least the ones that cost your child marks. A pencil 6 that looks like a b
is exactly the thing worth overruling.

## Marking it yourself instead

Open the paper from your child's results. Under each written answer there is a
marks row: type the mark, add a comment, save. Tap a photograph to open it full
screen and zoom in — you cannot mark what you cannot read.

Multiple-choice and clear short answers are already marked when you arrive; that
is why marking happens inside the paper rather than on a list of loose answers.

Press **Release** in the crumb row when you are done.

## If it stops part-way

The whole paper is reserved before anything is spent, so a paper you cannot
afford is refused up front rather than half-marked. If an answer fails for some
other reason, the rest still release — what was marked is worth reading, and a
paper held shut by one timeout is the dead end this exists to remove.

Pressing the button again costs nothing for answers already marked.

## A child linked from a teacher

A child you linked with a teacher's code is marked by that teacher. The teacher
can let you mark too, from the child's report on their side. Once they have:

- **Mark N & release** appears on your child's papers, as it does for a child
  you created.
- Opening a paper gives you the marks row under each written answer,
  **Assess with AI**, and **Release** — the same screen the teacher marks on.
- Any AI marking comes out of **your own credits**, never the teacher's.
- The teacher can still mark and release the paper themselves. Whoever saves a
  mark last is the mark your child sees, and each mark records who gave it.

If the teacher switches it off again, your marks stay as they are and you are
back to watching. Until it is on, opening a paper only shows it to you once it
has been released.

## Releasing without marking

You can release a paper at any time from inside it. Your child then sees the
correct answers and worked solutions, with the unmarked questions still blank.
Sometimes that is what you want — the solutions are the point, not the score.
