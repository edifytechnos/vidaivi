## What a credit buys

One credit marks **one answer** with AI. You start with
**{{rules.parentTrialCredits}} credits** and they do not expire at the end of the
month — a parent's credits are a balance, not an allowance that resets.

## What a paper costs

One credit for every question that needs a marker:

| Question type | Credits |
|---|---|
| Multiple choice | 0 — marked instantly, always free |
| Short answer | 1 |
| Long answer (photographed working) | 1 |

A fifteen-question chapter test with seven written answers costs **7**.

That is the worst case on purpose. A short answer that is plainly a number gets
marked automatically and spends nothing — so you are often charged less than the
number you were quoted, never more.

## Why your child's test can say "not available yet"

Before a child starts a paper, the app checks you could afford to mark the whole
thing. If the paper costs 7 and you have 5 left, the test is **locked** — they
cannot start it.

This is deliberate. A child who fills in fifteen questions and then finds nobody
can mark them has wasted an evening. Better to stop at the door.

**Your child is never told it is about money.** The tile says only *not available
yet*, and if they tap it they are told to ask whoever set it up. The number is on
your screen, not theirs.

To unlock it: [top up](05-buying.html), or mark and release something else first
to free nothing — credits spent are spent — so realistically, top up.

## Watching the balance

It sits above the list on your Children screen. Below a fifth of your grant it
turns amber and says what to do, rather than waiting until it refuses.

If the balance fails to load it shows nothing at all, rather than guessing. The
real limit is on the server; a blank is a network blip, not a zero.

## Papers you mark by hand

Marking by hand is **always free** and always available, credits or no credits.
If you run out mid-term you can still open every paper, award every mark yourself
and release it. What runs out is only the AI first draft.
