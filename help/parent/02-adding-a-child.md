## Two ways, and they are not the same

**Add a child** on the Children screen asks which one you want.

### Create a login

You issue the username and the password. This child is **yours**: you choose
what they practise, you mark their papers, and you can reset their password.

The password is shown **once**, on the screen, with a Copy button. It is stored
scrambled and cannot be read back afterwards — copy it into WhatsApp before you
close the dialog. If you lose it, reset it; that costs nothing but signs the
child out of their devices.

### Link a child a teacher registered

Your child's teacher gives you a one-time code. Redeeming it links that child to
your account so you can **watch** — their papers, their marks, their progress.

You cannot mark a linked child's paper, and you cannot mark it with AI either.
That paper belongs to the teacher who issued the login, and two people marking
the same answer is worse than one.

## How many

{{rules.parentMaxChildren}} on a parent account. Children you create count
against that; children linked from a teacher do not use your practice.

## Telling them apart

On the Children screen, a child you created is **yours to mark**. A linked child
is marked *linked* — you only watch.

## Resetting a password

Open the child, press **Reset password**, and the new one is shown once, exactly
as at creation. Doing this signs that child out everywhere, which is the point if
somebody else has the old one.

## Removing a child

Removing deletes the login, their attempts, their marks **and the photographs of
their handwriting**. It cannot be undone. Do it when a child genuinely leaves —
not to free a slot you want back for a week.
