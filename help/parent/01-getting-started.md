## Signing in

Press **Continue with Google** on [vidai.seyali.app](/). The first time, two
things are asked once and never again:

1. **Are you a teacher or a parent?** Pick **parent**. This decides the shape of
   the whole app for you, and it cannot be changed afterwards without an admin
   resetting your account — so read both tiles before tapping.
2. **A WhatsApp number.** This is how you get sent your child's score reports.

## What a parent account is for

A child normally hands their paper to a teacher. If there is no teacher behind
your child, the paper would sit unmarked forever — so as a parent you are the
marker. You get:

- your own subjects and tests, so you can put practice in front of your child;
- up to **{{rules.parentMaxChildren}} children**;
- **{{rules.parentTrialCredits}} AI credits** to start with, which is what marks
  a paper for you in one press.

## Your trial

A new account gets **{{rules.trialDays}} days** with
{{rules.trialSubjects}} subject, {{rules.trialTests}} tests of your own and
{{rules.trialStudents}} children. You can also take
**{{rules.freeShelfTests}} tests** out of the ready-made library free, to see
whether it suits your child, before buying a whole subject for
{{rules.subjectPaise.rupees}}.

## The order it goes in

1. [Add a child](02-adding-a-child.html) — a login you give them.
2. Pick practice: take a ready-made subject, or write your own.
3. Your child sits the test and hands it in.
4. [Mark it and release it](04-marking-and-releasing.html) — one press.
5. They read their marks, the correct answers and the worked solutions.

## Where things are

- **Children** — your children, their papers, and your credit balance.
- **Subjects** — the practice you own.
- **Browse tests** — the ready-made library, to read before you take anything.
- **To mark** — papers waiting on you.
