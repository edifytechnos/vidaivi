---
title: Putting Vidai on your phone
order: 6
summary: Install Vidai like an app on your phone, so it opens from its own icon, full screen.
---

## On an Android phone

1. Open [vidai.seyali.app](/) in **Chrome**.
2. Press **Install the app** under the sign-in buttons, or in the menu under
   your name once you are signed in. If you do not see it,
   open Chrome's menu (the three dots) and choose **Install app** or
   **Add to Home screen**.
3. Press **Install**.

Vidai now has its own icon on your home screen, like any other app.

## On an iPhone

1. Open [vidai.seyali.app](/) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## What changes, and what does not

- It opens straight into Vidai, full screen, with no address bar.
- On Android you stay signed in. On an iPhone the app keeps its own sign-in,
  separate from Safari's, so sign in once more the first time you open it.
- It still needs the internet. Without it you see a short page saying so, with
  **Try again**. Answers you have already saved are safe.
- It updates itself. There is nothing to download when Vidai changes.
