## What is for sale

| | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| A ready-made subject | {{rules.subjectPaise.rupees}} | Every chapter test in it, copied into your account, yours to edit |
| A credit pack | {{rules.creditPackPaise.rupees}} | {{rules.creditPackCredits}} AI marking credits added to your balance |

Before buying a subject you can take **{{rules.freeShelfTests}} tests** out of the
library free. Use them — a chapter your child has not reached is a poor test of
whether the subject suits them.

## How paying works

1. You reach the payment dialog — from the price when a limit refuses you, from
   **Top up** beside your credit balance, or from Browse.
2. If you have a discount code, type it and press Apply. The price above it
   changes.
3. Press pay.
   - **On a phone**, the UPI link opens GPay or PhonePe with the amount, the
     payee and the reference already filled in. Do not change the amount — the
     reference is how your payment is matched to your order.
   - **On a laptop**, copy the UPI ID shown and pay from your own phone, putting
     the reference in the note.
4. Come back and press **I have paid**.
5. An admin checks the payment arrived and confirms it. Your subject or your
   credits appear then — usually within a day.

## Closing the dialog cancels the order

If you close without paying, the order is cancelled. That is on purpose;
abandoned orders would otherwise pile up against your account. Just start again.

## Discount codes

A code takes a percentage off, a flat amount off, or both. A code that is not
recognised is answered with the ordinary price and a sentence saying so — never
an error, so a typo is not a wall.

## Credits added to a balance you already have

A top-up **adds**. It never wipes what is left and never forgives what you have
already spent. Buying a pack on a balance of 12 leaves you with
{{rules.creditPackCredits}} more than you had.

## If Buy is nowhere on your screen

Payments are switched off until the platform's UPI details are configured. There
is nothing wrong with your account — ask whoever runs your Vidai.

## What is recorded

The order keeps what you actually paid, the list price, the code you used and the
reference. If a price ever needs explaining six months later, it is there.
