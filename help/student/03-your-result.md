## Why the paper is silent at first

You handed in; nothing came back. That is deliberate. Long answers need a person
to mark them, and a score that counts only the multiple-choice questions would be
a half-truth. So the paper stays shut until whoever marks it presses **Release**.

Until then, your saved results list says **"Handed in · waiting for your
teacher"**.

## When it opens

The row becomes a button. Open it and you get the paper one question at a time,
the same shape you sat it in, with three things added:

- **Your marks** on each question, and the total at the top.
- **The correct answer**, next to what you put.
- **The worked solution** — the full method, beside the question.

If somebody marked a long answer by hand they may have left a comment. It sits
under the mark.

## The thing actually worth doing

Read the worked solution for every question you got **right** as well. On a board
paper the method carries the marks, and a right answer reached the long way round
is the one that runs out of time in the exam hall.

## Trying again

**Retake test** appears on a released paper. Two things to know:

- Ready-made library tests can be sat **{{rules.subjectAttempts}} times**. After
  that the retake button goes, but you keep your marks, your answers and every
  worked solution — that part never expires.
- A test your own teacher wrote has no limit unless they say so.

If you are out of attempts and need another, ask your teacher: they can grant you
one.

## Your saved results

The **Results** item in the menu lists every paper you have handed in, newest
first, with its score once released. That is the record — it follows your
account, not the phone you sat it on.
