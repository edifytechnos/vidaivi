## Where your login comes from

You do not sign up. Whoever set you up — your teacher, or a parent — creates the
login and hands it to you, usually on WhatsApp. It looks like a name and a short
password made of two or three ordinary words and a number.

## Signing in

1. Open [vidai.seyali.app](/).
2. Press **Student sign in**.
3. Type the username and the password exactly as they were given to you. They
   are all lowercase.

If it says *"Wrong username or password"*, check for a stray space at the end —
phones like to add one after a pasted word.

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in for about a month, on that phone, until you sign out. You do
not have to sign in again for each test.

If you get signed out unexpectedly, it is usually one of two things: somebody
reset your password, or you signed out everywhere from another device. Ask
whoever gave you the login for a new password.

## Forgot the password

There is no reset link — nobody at Vidai holds your password, it is stored
scrambled. Ask your teacher or your parent to reset it. They will see a new one
on their screen and can send it to you. Your old marks and papers are untouched.

## Where you land

Your **subjects**. Each one is a folder of tests. Open a subject to see what is
waiting for you.
