## Opening it

From your subject, tap a test. You get one question, with the list of all the
questions beside it — on a phone that list is behind the **Questions** button at
the top.

You can answer them in any order, and go back and change any of them. An
answered question gets a tick in the list.

## There is no Save button

Your answer saves itself:

- **Multiple choice** — tapping the option is the save.
- **Short answer** — it saves about a second after you stop typing, or straight
  away when you press Enter or tap somewhere else.
- **Long answer** — the photo saves when it finishes uploading.

If you want to take an answer back off the paper and leave the question for
later, use **Clear answer** at the top of the answer box.

## Long answers: photograph your working

A long answer is marked by a person, so they need to see your working, not just
your final line.

1. Do the question on paper as you normally would.
2. Tap **Add photo** and either take one there and then or pick one you already
   took.
3. Check it is readable — tap the photo to open it full screen and zoom in. If
   it is blurry or the page is cut off, remove it with the ✕ and take it again.

You can add up to three photos per answer. Write clearly and photograph straight
on, in good light; a mark you lose because a minus sign is invisible is a mark
nobody can give back.

## Short answers do not have to be numbers

You can type a symbol — `root 3 / 2`, `pi/4`, `x = 2`. If the marker cannot read
it as right or wrong automatically, it goes to whoever marks your paper rather
than being counted wrong. You never lose a mark because of how you wrote it.

## Maths on the screen

Matrices, integrals and fractions are drawn properly. If a formula ever looks
like raw code, reload the page — that is a loading problem, not the question.

## Handing in

**Hand in test** is at the top of the screen. Once you hand in:

- you go back to your subject, with a note saying the paper is in;
- you can still open the paper and read what you answered;
- you cannot change anything.

## What you see afterwards

**Nothing yet, and that is on purpose.** No score, no correct answers, no worked
solutions, until your teacher or your parent has marked it and released it.

See [reading your result](03-your-result.html).

## Only one test at a time

While a paper is part-way through, your other tests are locked. Finish or hand
in the one you are on and they unlock.

## Something went wrong

- **"Not available yet" on a test tile** — it is not ready for you. Ask whoever
  set it up; they can see why.
- **You closed the tab mid-test** — nothing is lost. Open the test again and you
  land back on the same question.
- **The photo will not upload** — it may be too large. Take it again rather than
  picking a huge one from the gallery; the app shrinks a fresh photo for you.
