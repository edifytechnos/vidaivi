## Where they live

**Plans & pricing**. Changing one changes what the next person is quoted, within
about a minute. Nothing needs a deploy, because the first thing that happens to a
price is that it changes.

> A limit of **0 means no limit**, not "none allowed". That is how an unlimited
> plan is written.

## What is actually billed today

The **one-off subject** and the **credit packs**. The teacher's monthly fee and
the per-seat price are counted, priced and shown, but no subscription is charged
— renewal, expiry and what happens to a roster when it lapses are materially more
than a one-off unlock.

Until then, **paid seats** and **free forever** on the accounts list are how a
real arrangement is honoured.

## Where a limit bites

Four places, and each refusal names what was hit and what lifts it:

- Creating a subject.
- Creating a test.
- Taking a copy from the library — this one names the price and opens the
  payment dialog.
- Creating a student — plus, for a teacher, the allowlist.

A bare "no" reads as a broken product. A number and a price is a decision.
