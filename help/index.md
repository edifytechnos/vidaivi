## What Vidai is

A teacher (or a parent) puts a practice paper in front of a student. The student
sits it on a phone, answers question by question, photographs their working for
the long answers and hands it in. Somebody marks it — by hand, or with an AI
first draft — and then **releases** it, and only then does the student see their
marks, the correct answers and the worked solutions.

That last step is the whole idea. A test is silent until it is released.
