# Tamil Nadu Class 12 (HSE II) — provenance

Fetched 2026-09-25 from the DGE Tamil Nadu question bank index
(https://apply1.tndge.org/dge-notification/questbank, linked from dge.tn.gov.in).
DGE publishes one combined "all subjects" PDF per sitting; each file here is a
page range cut from it, unmodified.

The papers do not print the year themselves. The year comes from DGE's own
label on the combined PDF, and each year uses a distinct Q.P. code series
(66xx / 74xx / 83xx), so the three cannot be confused.

| Year | DGE label | Combined PDF | Q.P. codes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 2023 MAR HR SEC SECOND YEAR ALL SUBJECT QUESTION PAPER | notification/questbank/2023_Mar_HSE_II.pdf | Maths 6612, Physics 6617, Chemistry 6622, Biology 6627 |
| 2024 | 2024 - MARCH - HR.SEC. SECOND YEAR ALL SUBJECT QUESTION PAPER | notification/questbank/2200420242.pdf | Maths 7412, Physics 7417, Chemistry 7422, Biology 7427 |
| 2025 | 2025 MARCH HR.SEC. SECOND YEAR ALL SUBJECT QUESTION PAPER | notification/questbank/2025_MAR_12_QUESTION_PAPER.pdf | Maths 8312, Physics 8317, Chemistry 8322, Biology 8327 |

All are the March main sitting, Tamil & English bilingual.

`bio-botany-*` and `bio-zoology-*` are Part I and Part II of the single
**Biology** paper (x627), split at the "PART - II (BIO-ZOOLOGY)" page. The
Bio-Zoology file keeps the Biology cover page (instructions, time, marks).
The separate pure-science **Botany** (x632) and **Zoology** (x637) papers are
not included.
